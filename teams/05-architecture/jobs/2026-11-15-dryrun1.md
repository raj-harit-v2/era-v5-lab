## How the run is scored and reported

- Bits per byte divides the summed next-token loss by the original UTF-8 byte count of `heldout_v1`: 1,059 documents, 3,775,551 bytes. C3 normalizes inside the tokenizer. 74 documents change under that normalizer and stay in the score.
- Every slice is reported. The three-group mean is English, code, and the mean of the 11 Indic slices. Math and science are reported beside it.
- This is one run at one seed. It does not name a winner and it does not change `config_v1.yaml`.
- The loss curve and any spikes are recorded in `teams/05-architecture/experiments/results/dryrun1_loss.md` by its owner, from `metrics.jsonl`, `losses.txt` and the gradient norm of this run. That file is due Fri 20 Nov.
- Team 7 watches the run. The trainer writes one row to `metrics.jsonl` at every step and flushes the file every 10 steps. It runs the held-out pass at step 0, every 50 steps, and at the last step. The two intervals are from the checked-in `runtime.yaml`. I have not seen `runtime_fast.yaml`.
