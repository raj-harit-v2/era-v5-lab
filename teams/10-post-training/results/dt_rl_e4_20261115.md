# Deletion Tuning as the RL method on a 7B surrogate (E4, E4b), 15 Nov 2026

**Surrogate evidence, one seed.** Public Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-Instruct (dense), not the capstone model. Each table is
one run with one seed. This is the work-order question "can DT be the RL stage?", measured with our trainer
(`train.dt`), not a reproduction of the Deletion Tuning paper's RL recipe.

Drivers: `../rl-loop/pilots/fable_e1_20261115/` (`modal_app.py::e4`, `cfg_e4_dt.yaml`, `cfg_e4b_dt.yaml`).
Evidence: `fable_20261115_evidence/summary_e4a.json`, `summary_e4b.json`, `analysis_e4a.json`, `analysis_e4b.json`.
The comparison run E1 is in `code_rl_e1_20261115.md`. The preregistration (with three amendments, each written
before the steps it affects) is a local file and is not in the repository.

## Setup

Same model, cohort (the 56 prompts E1 screened), held-out set (96 x 8), objective (objective_v1), batch (16 prompts x
8), max_new 1,024 and evaluations as E1. Trainable: 1.90 B gate_proj mask logits in 28 layers, nothing else. Official
GRPO settings of `agentic-learning-ai-lab/deleting-weights` @ `94f0340`: Wanda prior 1/2048 (prior strength 3,
calibrated on the 56 cohort prompts), alpha 1,000 rising to 10,000 over 100 steps, mask lr 3e-4, lambda 1e-5. No KL or
entropy term, because objective_v1 has none. The sampler holds the hard-masked weights `W * 1[G > 0]`.

- Hardware: NVIDIA H200 (Modal); torch 2.13.0+cu130, transformers 5.19.0, vllm 0.32.0. Peak 89.5 GB, so it does not
  fit an 80 GB card (one fp32 logit, its gradient and two Adam moments per gate weight).
- Commands, from `rl-loop/pilots/fable_e1_20261115/` with `G10_E1_WORK` and `G10_E1_CAP_USD` set:

```bash
modal run --detach modal_app.py::e4 --run-id e4a --steps 30 --budget-s 3600 --grace-s 600 --max-calls 1 --train-ids-file <private folder>/e1/private/cohort_ids_e1a.txt
modal run --detach modal_app.py::e4 --run-id e4a --steps 60 --budget-s 2400 --grace-s 600 --max-calls 1 --train-ids-file <private folder>/e1/private/cohort_ids_e1a.txt
modal run --detach modal_app.py::e4 --run-id e4b --steps 20 --budget-s 1500 --grace-s 300 --max-calls 1 --config cfg_e4b_dt.yaml --train-ids-file <private folder>/e1/private/cohort_ids_e1a.txt
```

- Sync check passed (deleting one whole layer: K3 0.072 unsynced, 0.0007 synced).

| Step | E4 held-out | E1 held-out | E4 cohort | E1 cohort |

| 20 | 0.154 | 0.253 | 0.413 | 0.768 |
| 30 | 0.155 | 0.242 | 0.397 | 0.839 |
| 40 | 0.146 | 0.242 | 0.406 | 0.884 |
| 50 | 0.163 | 0.243 | 0.402 | 0.926 |
| 60 | 0.167 | 0.251 | 0.397 | 0.944 |

*Surrogate model, one seed per run. Paired bootstrap over problems, 10,000 resamples.*

- **Held-out, step 0 to 60: +0.020, 95% CI [-0.008, +0.048]. Cohort: +0.049, CI [-0.020, +0.118].** Neither interval
  excludes zero (the cohort's does at step 40 only: +0.058 [+0.007, +0.109]). Training reward drifted up slowly
  (0.366 mean over the first ten steps, 0.452 over the last ten).
- The mask hardly changed: deleted weights went from 3.43 M to 3.07 M of 1.90 B (about a tenth of the initial
  deletions were restored), and only 8.8% of logits ever changed value. The share of logits with a non-zero gradient
  fell from 9% to under 1% as the sharpness rose. Held-out reasons are flat (runtime errors 32.3% to 34.2%).
- Step-0 baselines differ slightly from E1's because the Wanda prior deletes 0.18% of gate weights before step 0 and
  the samples are fresh (held-out 0.147 vs 0.151, cohort 0.348 vs 0.299).
- Preregistered reading: **"DT too slow at this scale"**. This is a statement about 128 rollouts per step and 60
  updates with the official settings. The paper's recipe uses 2,048 rollouts per step; nothing here tests that.
- Mechanics: 31 s per step, trust-region mask inert (1e-4), engine gap K3 at most 3.7e-4, state 7.6 GB. Extended from
  30 to 60 steps by resume.
- First use of the subgroup observer: over the run Aizu was 22.2% of sampled groups and 25.8% of kept groups; on a
  pre-screened cohort that does not saturate, group dropping barely shifts the mixture.

## E4b: mask lr 10x (3e-3), 20 steps

Preregistration amendment 3, written after E4 and before this job. Identical to E4 except mask lr.

| Step | E4b held-out | E4b cohort | (E4 cohort) | (E1 cohort) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 0.147 | 0.348 | 0.348 | 0.299 |
| 10 | 0.181 | 0.491 | 0.371 | 0.645 |
| 20 | 0.180 | 0.491 | 0.413 | 0.768 |

*Surrogate model, one seed.*

- **Cohort +0.143, 95% CI [+0.085, +0.203]; held-out +0.033, CI [+0.007, +0.061].** Both exclude zero.
- Deleted weights went from 3.49 M to 4.12 M (+18%), almost all in the first six steps; after that the count and both
  curves are flat. The mask stops learning as the sharpness schedule (1,000 rising to 2,800 by step 20) pushes the
  logits out of the band where they have a gradient.
- The preregistered "rate-limited" reading held: at the official lr the mask could not move within the window, at
  10x it did and the reward rose. The gain is about a third of attention-weight RL's at the same step (cohort +0.14
  vs +0.35 at step 10), and it does not continue.
- On held-out the change is again fewer runtime errors (32.3% to 26.6%), with wrong answers not falling.

Final useful output (`summary_e4b.json`): `"deletion_final": {"deleted": 4121498, ...}, "alpha_final": 2800.0,
"optimizer_steps": 20`, `"peak_gpu_memory_gb": 89.52`.

What this gives the DT work order: a deletion mask alone, trained by our RL loop on a real model, raises the code
reward on trained and held-out prompts, by much less than attention-weight RL and only until the sharpness freezes it.

## Code

`rl-loop/train.py` `train.dt` (needs `algo: objective_v1`, an hf model and no `train.trainable`; mask logits are the
only trainable tensors; hard-mask weights are what the sampler holds; DT-specific vLLM sync check; official
regulariser once per optimizer step; the lost-update rule is off by default for DT because saturated logits rightly do
not move, and the shares are still logged). Tests: `rl-loop/test_dt_rl.py`, `rl-loop/test_sampler_branches.py`.

## Unresolved and limits

- **Two E4 launches failed within two minutes each** before the first good call ($0.23 and $0.28): a file missing from
  the job image (`FileNotFoundError: '/root/team/dt/mask.py'`), then a misplaced log line in the sampler-only branch that no CPU test had run. Added
  because of it: the CPU dry run `modal_app.py::smoke` in the same image before every GPU call of `e1`/`e4`
  (standalone `::dryrun`), `rl-loop/test_sampler_branches.py` (stand-in sampler engines exercise the vLLM-only
  branches on CPU), and a local watchdog (`watch_run.py`, not in Git) that stops the app on a crash or stall. The
  later calls passed the dry run first; the watchdog raised no alert.
- One seed per arm; no learning-rate sweep beyond 3e-4 and 3e-3; no slower sharpness schedule; batch 128 rollouts
  per step against the paper's 2,048.
- The cohort was screened by the base model for E1, not for DT; both runs share it on purpose for the comparison.

## Cost (ledger estimate, not billing)

E4: $6.07 including the two failed launches. E4b: $2.11. CPU dry runs: under $0.01 in total.

## Not measured

A slower or fixed sharpness schedule (the obvious next lever: alpha held at 1,000, mask lr 3e-3), other targets than
gate_proj, other learning rates, a second seed, a batch near the paper's, the capstone model, and DT-then-RL (DT SFT
followed by RL).

## Addendum, 16 Nov 2026: E7, E4b with the sharpness held constant

**Surrogate evidence, one seed.** Same model, cohort, held-out set, objective, batch and evaluations as E4b; the only
change is `alpha_end: 1000.0`, so alpha stays at 1,000 instead of reaching 2,800 by step 20. Config
`../rl-loop/pilots/fable_e1_20261115/cfg_e7_dt_const.yaml`. Evidence: `fable_20261115_evidence/summary_e7a.json`,
`analysis_e7a.json`, `analysis_e7_vs_e4b.json`. The preregistration (written before the job, with one amendment
after it) is a local file and is not in the repository.

Question: E4b's mask and reward froze after about six to ten updates while the rising sharpness closed the gradient
band. With the sharpness fixed, do they keep moving?

```bash
modal run --detach modal_app.py::e4 --run-id e7a --steps 20 --budget-s 1080 --grace-s 180 --pad-s 90 --max-calls 1 --config cfg_e7_dt_const.yaml --train-ids-file <private folder>/e1/private/cohort_ids_e1a.txt
```

Hardware: one NVIDIA H200 (Modal), same versions as E4b. The CPU dry run in the job image passed before the GPU
call. Function time 1,174 s; 31.4 s per step after step 1; peak 91.3 GB. The vLLM sync check, both step-0 evaluations
and the step-1 rollouts are identical to E4b's, so the two runs differ only from the first update on.

| Step | E7 cohort | E4b cohort | E7 held-out | E4b held-out | E7 gross mask flips in the step |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 0.348 | 0.348 | 0.147 | 0.147 | |
| 1 | | | | | 1,952,128 |
| 10 | 0.458 | 0.491 | 0.158 | 0.181 | 49,256 |
| 20 | 0.511 | 0.491 | 0.158 | 0.180 | 9,815 |

*Surrogate model (dense, not the capstone MoE), one seed per run, 16 prompts x 8 rollouts per step, 56 trained
cohort prompts, 96 held-out problems. Paired bootstrap over problems, 10,000 resamples. Gross flips are newly
deleted plus restored weights, counted exactly over the 1.90 B mask logits.*

- **Cohort step 0 to 20: +0.163, 95% CI [+0.094, +0.232]. Difference to E4b on the same problems: +0.020
  [-0.036, +0.074].** Held-out: +0.010 [-0.022, +0.044]; difference to E4b -0.022 [-0.055, +0.008]. The cohort rose
  between steps 10 and 20 (+0.054 [+0.005, +0.103]) where E4b's was flat, but it started lower at step 10 and ended
  level with E4b.
- The gradient band stayed open: 8.7% to 9.1% of logits kept a nonzero FP32 gradient (E4b fell to 0.24%). The mask
  still slowed down: flips fell roughly geometrically, restored was 60% to 75% of newly deleted, and the share of
  logits with a soft mask between 0.001 and 0.999 fell from 0.79% to 0.08%. The updates push logits away from zero in
  both directions, so the sharpness schedule was not the main brake.
- No numerical gap from the softer mask: the soft-versus-hard ratio left [0.8, 1.2] on at most 3.4e-4 of tokens, and
  the trust region masked at most 3.8e-4 (E4b 3.5e-4).
- Reading: the mask keeps moving a little, the reward does not follow beyond E4b. For the DT work order this
  confirms E4b's picture (a mask-only policy raises the reward for about ten updates and then stalls) and removes
  the constant-sharpness lever as a fix at 128 rollouts per step.

Final useful output (`summary_e7a.json`): `"status": "COMPLETE"`, `"deletion_final": {"deleted": 4211053, ...}`,
`"alpha_final": 1000.0`, `"optimizer_steps": 20`, `"peak_gpu_memory_gb": 91.29`, `"vllm_sync_check": {..., "ok": true}`.

New per-step logging used here (`train.dt` runs only; other runs are unchanged): `dt_newly_deleted`, `dt_restored`,
`dt_live_share`, `dt_band_share`, `dt_soft_hard_gap`, and `sh_k3`, `sh_abs_logratio`, `sh_outside_frac` (the
trainer's soft-mask log-probs against its own hard-mask log-probs on the step's tokens, before the update; the
existing `mismatch_k3` is the HF-versus-vLLM gap with the hard mask on both sides). It costs no extra forward pass;
it keeps one boolean copy of the hard mask (1.9 GB on this model). Tests: `rl-loop/test_dt_rl.py` (seven new).

Cost: $2.28 ledger estimate for the GPU call and two CPU dry runs; Modal billing rose by $2.45 over the same period.

Not measured: completions were not read (the trainer stores scores and failure reasons only); a second seed; other
learning rates; a batch near the paper's 2,048 rollouts per step; the capstone model.
