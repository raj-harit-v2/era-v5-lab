## 7. Gates that stay open (what these outputs are not)

- **Admission.** Only the four `allowed` sets are licence-eligible. `TSAI reserves` is not admission; its records are measurements. Four sets are held for a data-rule ruling (section 1). OCR-2 is the WP1 pack and is not counted here. No row of any set here is admitted to a training pack by this work.
- **Decontamination.** None of these outputs has been through Team 9's checker at the pinned revisions. The 6 Nov SFT records on this machine are samples of older conversions (prefix rows, tokenizer v0.1, path-based ids), and the full-set kept-row records are in private run folders that are not here; neither is applied to these files, and sample counts do not establish protection for a full set. Gate: run `decontam_run.py` on each converted output (ids are `<set>/<source row index>` at the pinned revision) and exclude the flagged ids before any pack. Until then every full output is **converted, not decontaminated**.
- **Held-out ids.** No id list exists for these sets; the WP15 lists cover OCR-2 shards only. A function-calling held-out list (xLAM) would need the set to leave `hold` first. Gate: exclude any list by id at the same revision before a pack.
- **Length.** Rows over 32,768 tokens are counted, not removed or silently admitted. A converted file that contains them is not a train-ready 32k pack. The pack step must drop them (`max_sequence_tokens` 32,768, provisional) and report the count per set; at this prefix scope that removes 445 of 469 `swe-rebench` rows, all 34 `proofs-v3-sft` rows and 144 of 982 `math-v2` rows.
- **Decode contract.** The check requires `decode(input_ids)` to equal the text, or to equal the text after the C3 normaliser (NFC, plus the Malayalam chillu rewrite). It records how many rows needed the normaliser. It does not claim raw-byte-exact decoding for every row, and the frozen C3 normaliser is not changed.
- **Scope of the first-1,000 numbers.** They are prefixes of source order. Do not read effort mix, length percentiles or the over-length rate as properties of the whole set.
